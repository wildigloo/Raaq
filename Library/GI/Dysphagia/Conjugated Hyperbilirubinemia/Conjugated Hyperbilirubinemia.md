# Conjugated Hyperbilirubinemia [1]
*Last edited by Adem Berat Dalkilic on 8/15/26*

## Differential Diagnosis
### Adults
1. Cholelithiasis
2. Pancreatitis (chronic and acute)
3. Pancreatic or biliary cancers
4. Portal adenopathy
5. Biliary strictures following a procedure
6. Primary sclerosing cholangitis and Primary biliary cholangitis
7. AIDS cholangiopathy
8. Choledochal cyst
9. Sphincter of Oddi dysfunction
10. Parasites
11. Eosinophilic granuloma
12. Periampullary diverticulum
13. Mirizzi syndrome
14. Viral hepatitis (Heptatis A-E, CMV, EBV)
15. Alcoholic hepatitis
16. Nonalcoholi steatohepatitis
17. Cirrhosis
18. Ischemic hepatopathy
19. Drugs
20. Toxins
21. Autoimmune hepatitis
22. Systemic infection
23. Total parenteral nutrition
24. Postoperative jaundice
25. Sickle cell disease
26. Budd-Chiari syndrome
27. Parasites
28. Liver infiltration
29. Intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy
30. Organ transplantation
31. Sarcoidosis
32. Amyloidosis
33. Dubin-Johnson syndrome
34. Rotor's syndrome
35. Benign recurrent cholestasis
36. Progressive familial intrahepatic cholestasis
37. Caroli disease
38. Stauffer's syndrome
39. Wilson disease
40. Biliary atresia
41. Choledochal cyst
42. Idiopathic neonatal hepatitis
43. Caroli disease
44. Tyrosinemia
45. Gaucher disease
46. Niemann-Pick disease
47. Galactosemia
48. Alpha-1-antitrypsin deficiency
49. Sepsis
50. TORCH infections
51. Budd-Chiari
52. Wilson's
