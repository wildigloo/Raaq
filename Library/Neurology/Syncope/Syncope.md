# Syncope
*Last updated by Adem Berat Dalkilic on 8/11/26*

## Classification
- Cardiac syncope: syncope due to arrhythmias or structural heart disease
- Noncardiac syncope: self-explanatory

### Quick ddx
Cardiac: *VAV Sick, Stoked Supramen Twist points!; MI (my) aorta might pull the sep tam*
Noncardiac: *Vast situations require carots!; Neu POTS by the Old Bed need blood and water*
Mimicks: *Ventilate when exercising under the sun; Stealing glucose will make you drop into a seizure*

### Differential for Cardiac Syncope

- Arrhythmogenic syncope: brady/tachycardia causes reduced ejection fraction
	- Sick sinus syndrome
	- V-tach
	- AV block
	- Supraventricular arrhythmias
	- Stokes-Adams syndrome
	- Torsades de pointes (and meds that prolong QT)
- Cardiovascular syncope: myocardial dysfunction causes cardiac outflow obstruction
	- Massive MI
	- Aortic stenosis
	- Mitral valve prolapse
	- Atherosclerotic cardiovascular diseases
	- Pulmonary embolism
	- Pulmonary hypertension
	- Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (severe asymmetric spetal hypertrophy)
	- Cardiac tamponade

### Differential for Noncardiac Syncope
- Reflex syncopes
	- Vasovagal syncope (neurocardiogenic syndrome)
	- Situational syncope
	- Carotid sinus syndrome
- Orthostatic syncopes
	- Postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome
	- Neurogenic orthostatic hypotension (DM, Parkinson's)
	- Age-related loss of baroreceptor sensitivity
	- Anemia
	- Prolonged bed rest
	- Medication (vasodilators or heart rate reducers)
	- Hypovolemia
- Other and Mimicks
	- Exercise associated postural hypotension
	- Drop attacks
	- Acute hyperventilation syndrome
	- Heatstroke
	- Traumatic brain injury
	- Hypoglycemia
	- Subclavian steal syndrome
	- Psychogenic pseudosyncope
	- Seizure

## Differentiating the Types of Syncope

Prodrome:
	- Cardiac: none
	- Noncardiac:
		- Vasovagal: impaired senses, nausea, pallor, warmth, diaphoresis, hyperventilation
		- Orthostatic: nausea
