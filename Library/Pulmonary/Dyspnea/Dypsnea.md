*Last updated by: Adem Berat Dalkilic on 8/2/2026*

## Definition
Dyspnea itself is defined as a "subjective experience of breathing discomfort that comprises qualitative distinct sensations that vary in intensity"
Chronic dyspnea is defined as dyspnea persisting for more than one month.

## Essential Dyspnea *Kit*
1. Smoking history
2. Cardiac evaluation: EKG, echo, BNP
3. Pulmonary evaluation: chest X-ray and spirometry - look for restrictive changes vs obstructive changes
4. Revised Borg scale: allows evaluation of improvement of dyspnea and fatigue
5. 6-minute walk test: is predictive of prognosis in patients with COPD and heart failure
